Executive Summary

Company formation in France is the structured process through which a business presence is legally created, documented and made capable of operating within the French commercial and regulatory system. It covers the choice of legal form, drafting of statutes, registration via the one-stop formalities portal, entry in the trade and companies register and national company register, and core tax registrations needed before regular trading can begin. [web:20][web:24][web:29]

Operationally, company formation often starts with a decision about whether the business should be carried out through a limited liability company (such as SARL or SAS), a public limited company (SA), an individual enterprise (EI), micro-entrepreneur status or branch registration for a foreign enterprise. Founders assess liability, capital requirements, ownership flexibility, governance and administrative expectations before designing the legal structure that will hold contracts, assets and staff. [web:22][web:23][web:25][web:26][web:27]

The institutional environment is shaped by the central online business formalities portal, commercial court registries that maintain the Trade and Companies Register (Registre du commerce et des sociétés — RCS), the National Register of Companies managed through INPI, and French tax authorities. The one-stop portal collects information and forwards it to relevant bodies so that the company can be registered, assigned identification numbers (such as SIREN and SIRET) and onboarded for VAT and other tax relationships. [web:20][web:24][web:29][web:30][web:33]

Cross-border relevance is high because many French entities involve foreign founders or operate within international groups. Foreign companies may use French subsidiaries or branches and must consider tax liability, regulatory conditions and documentation requirements when entering France. Practical company formation decisions therefore often integrate French domestic rules with EU market context, international tax planning and group governance design. [web:21][web:22][web:31][web:34]
